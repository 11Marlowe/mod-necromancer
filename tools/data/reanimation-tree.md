## Reanimation Spellbook (WIP)

***General note:*** Only Humanoids, Undead, Demons, Beasts, Giants and Dragonkin can be raised. There are four types of items that can be harvested from corpses into your Corpse Bag:
* Lump of flesh: from any corporeal enemies
* Pile of bones: from any skeletal enemies
* Ectoplasm: from any spectral enemies
* Banshee essence: from female humanoids and banshees exclusively

***

***Level 1 - Bone Armor:*** Surrounds you with 4 Bone Shields, reducing physical damage taken by X. Each melee or ranged hit against you expends a charge. While the shields hold, your pushback time from spells is reduced by 70%. ***(Instant, Self-Buff, No CD)***

***

***Level 10 (Quest) - Raise Zombie:*** Raise a Zombie from an enemy corpse that will fight alongside you for 1 min. Requires a Humanoid or Zombie corpse. ***(5 second cast time, 20 yard range, No CD)***

***

***Level 10 (Quest) - Raise Shade:*** Raise a Shade from an enemy corpse that will fight alongside you for 1 min. Requires a Humanoid, Undead, Demon, Beast, Giant, or Dragonkin corpse. ***(3 second cast time, 20 yard range, No CD)***

***

***Level 10 (Quest) - Harvest:*** Harvest a corpse, storing it in your Corpse Bag and allowing it to be Raised at a later time. ***(Channeled for 3 sec, No CD)***

***

***Level 14 - Death Coil:*** Causes X to Y Shadow damage to an enemy or heals an Undead minion for X to Y health. ***(2.5 second cast time, 30 yard range, No CD)***

***

***Level 14 - Raise Ghoul:*** Raise a Ghoul from an enemy corpse that will fight alongside you for 1 min. Requires a Humanoid or Ghoul corpse. ***(5 second cast time, 20 yard range, No CD)***
* ***Note:*** Automatically available by trainer at level 14 if you have completed the level 10 class quest for Raise and Harvest.

***

***Level 14 - Raise Skeleton:*** Raise a Skeleton from an enemy corpse that will fight alongside you for 1 min. Requires a Humanoid or non-spectral Undead corpse. ***(5 sec cast time, 20 yard range, No CD)***
* ***Note:*** Automatically available by trainer at level 14 if you have completed the level 10 class quest for Raise and Harvest.

***

***Level 20 - Ghoul Armor:*** Increases shadow resistance by X and heals you for 3% of all damage you deal. ***(Instant, Self-Buff, No CD)***

***

***Level 20 (Talent) - Tether:*** Tethers an Undead minion to act as a pet with no limited duration. Tethered minions benefit from your increased attributes and allow control over their special abilities. Only one minion can be Tethered at a time. ***(Instant, 30 yard range, No CD)***

***

***Level 20 (Talent) - Sacrifice Minion:*** Sacrifices a summoned minion, healing you for X health and granting you a Death Pact effect. This effect is doubled if used on your Tethered minion. This effect is canceled upon Raising or Tethering a new minion. ***(Instant, 30 yard range, No CD)***
* Skeleton: Reduces all damage taken by 2%.
* Ghoul and Zombie: Increases physical damage done by 2%.
* Bloodmage: Increases spell damage done by 2%.
* Banshee: Restores 2% mana per 4 seconds.
* Shade: Reduces threat generated by 5%.
* Wraith: Increases melee and casting speed by 2%.
* Abomination: Increases your total health by 5%.

***

***Level 20 (Quest) - Raise Banshee:*** Raise a Banshee from an enemy corpse that will fight alongside you for 1 min. Requires a female Humanoid or Banshee corpse. ***(5 second cast time, 20 yard range, No CD)***

***

***Level 24 - Embalm:*** Reduces the damage received by your Tethered minion by 10% for 1 min. ***(Instant, 30 yard range, 3 min CD)***

***

***Level 26 - Lobotomize:*** Reduces the target's Intellect by X for 15 seconds. ***(Instant, 30 yard range, No CD)***

***

***Level 28 - Rule Undead:*** Incapacitates an enemy Undead target for up to 45 seconds. Any damage caused will revive the target. Only 1 target may be ruled at a time. ***(Instant, 30 yard range, No CD)***

***

***Level 30 (Talent) - Ghoul Frenzy:*** Sends your Tethered minion into a frenzy, breaking all crowd control effects, increasing its melee and casting speed by 25% for 30 sec and healing it for 50% of its health over the duration. ***(Instant, 1.5 min CD)***

***

***Level 30 (Talent) - Undead Domination:*** Your next Raise spell has its casting time reduced by 3 seconds and its Mana cost reduced by 60%. ***(Instant, 10 min CD)***

***

***Level 30 (Quest) - Raise Wraith:*** Raise a Wraith from an enemy corpse that will fight alongside you for 1 min. Requires a Humanoid, Undead, Demon, Beast, Giant, or Dragonkin corpse. Only 1 Wraith can be active at a time. ***(8 second cast time, 20 yard range, No CD)***

***

***Level 34 - Unholy Shield:*** Reduces all spell damage taken by 50% for 8 seconds and prevents the application of harmful magical effects. ***(Instant, Self-Buff, 3 min CD)***

***

***Level 38 (Item Drop) - Raise Bloodmage:*** Raise a Bloodmage from an enemy corpse that will fight alongside you for 1 min. Requires a Humanoid or non-spectral Undead corpse. Only 1 Bloodmage can be active at a time. ***(8 second cast time, 20 yard range, No CD)***
* ***Note:*** Book drops from Amnennar the Coldbringer in Razorfen Downs ***(10% drop chance)***

***

***Level 40 (Talent) - Raise Abomination:*** Raise an Abomination from three enemy corpses that will fight alongside you for 1 min. Requires Humanoid, Undead, or Giant corpses. Only 1 Abomination can be active at a time. ***(10 second cast time, 20 yard range, No CD)***

***

***Level 40 - Lich Armor:*** Increases spell critical strike by 3% and allows 15% of your mana regeneration to continue while casting. Additionally, enemies that strike you in melee may have their movement slowed by 30% for 5 sec. ***(Instant, Self-Buff, No CD)***

***

***Level 44 - Meat Catapult:*** Launch a corpse from your Corpse Bag, inflicting X to Y Shadow damage and stunning targets in a 3 yard radius for 3 seconds. Can be used while moving. ***(1.5 sec cast time, 20 yard range, 1 min CD)***

***

***Level 58 (Item Drop) - Raise Wight:*** Raise a Wight from three enemy corpses that will fight alongside you for 1 min. Requires Humanoid, Undead, or Giant corpses. Only 1 Wight or Abomination can be active at a time. ***(10 second cast time, 20 yard range, No CD)***
* ***Note 1:*** Book drops from Darkmaster Gandling in Scholomance ***(5% drop chance)***
* ***Note 2:*** Requires: Raise Abomination - Learned
* ***Note 3:*** Wights have near identical stats to Abominations, but a different set of special abilities.
